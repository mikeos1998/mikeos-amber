M.I.K.E - CONTROLS (PC)
=======================

1 PLAYER (and all the menus)
----------------------------
Move / menu up & down .... Arrow keys  or  W A S D
Jump / select ............ Space
Back ..................... X  or  Backspace
Credits (on title) ....... C
Pause .................... Enter
Turn the camera .......... Q and E


2 PLAYER RACE
-------------
                    PLAYER 1             PLAYER 2
Move .............. W A S D              Arrow keys
Jump .............. Z                    Space
Back .............. X                    Backspace
Pause ............. Tab                  Enter
Turn the camera ... Q and E              ,  and  .

On the character select screen, either player can press left/right to swap
characters. Z or Tab (player 1), or Space or Enter (player 2) starts the race.


LEVEL EDITOR (1 PLAYER > MY LEVELS)
-----------------------------------
Move the cursor .......... Arrow keys
Place the piece .......... Space
Erase .................... X
Next piece ............... C
Height down / up ......... Q and E
Editor menu .............. Enter  (test play, save, clear, exit)


M.I.K.E ON VMU (the pet window / PLAY IT HERE)
----------------------------------------------
Open / close the menu .... X (B)
Scroll the menu .......... Up / Down
Do it (A) ................ Space
Save and close ........... Esc (pet window)  /  Enter (play it here)


GAME CONTROLLERS (Xbox, PlayStation, etc.)
------------------------------------------
The 1st controller is player 1, the 2nd is player 2.
Move .............. Left stick or d-pad
Jump / select ..... A
Back .............. B
Credits ........... Y (on the title screen)
Pause ............. Start
Turn the camera ... Triggers or shoulder buttons


WINDOW
------
F11 or Alt+Enter .. Fullscreen on/off
Esc ............... Leave fullscreen
